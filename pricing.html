Questions before you choose.

What does the Free demo include?

Every feature is available on 10-artist scans, including edition comparison, Wishlist, similar artists, and advanced reports.

What does Standard leave out?

Standard tracks the whole library, with built-in reports and export as Markdown or CSV, or print. It leaves out advanced reports, the custom report builder, edition comparison, Wishlist, and similar artists. See the full tier comparison or Features.

Are the paid tiers subscriptions?

No. Standard, Pro, and Family are one-time purchases. Each has a 30-day refund.

How do I get a refund?

Paid tiers have a 30-day refund, no questions, through Lemon Squeezy. Use the receipt email or the Lemon Squeezy customer portal.

What is the difference between Pro and Family?

They share the same Pro feature depth. Pro activates two devices. Family activates five.

How do I activate or upgrade a paid tier?

Lemon Squeezy emails the key after checkout. In the app: Settings → License. Every tier uses the same Windows installer; the key changes the tier. Standard and Pro allow two devices. Family allows five.

Do I need a Missing Music account?

No. The app has no Missing Music cloud account. Your primary library data stays on your computer.

Is the Free demo a separate installer?

No. Every tier uses the same Windows installer. Without a key, it runs as the Free demo. Lemon Squeezy keys unlock paid tiers. Linux and macOS builds are coming soon.

Is this Lidarr, Picard, or SongKong?

No. Lidarr downloads missing albums. Picard and SongKong rewrite tags. Missing Music shows the gap, including edition differences, and leaves the library alone. We recommend Picard as an optional first tagging pass. We don’t bundle or recommend a downloader — this app never fetches music.

What if Windows SmartScreen blocks the installer?

Early builds are unsigned on purpose. Download only from the official GitHub Releases page, compare the SHA-256 hash with SHA256SUMS, then use More info and Run anyway if SmartScreen appears.
